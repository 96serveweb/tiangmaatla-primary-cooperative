From small garden to commercial farming cooperative

Tiangmaatla Primary Cooperative Limited was founded by community member Melida Matsi, emerging from the Tiangmaatla Multipurpose Centre’s door-to-door campaigns that revealed severe poverty and food insecurity in Sebayeng village, Limpopo. We are registered under the Cooperative Act, supplying maize, cabbage, onions, tomatoes, beetroot, carrots, potatoes, and poultry (broilers & layers).

Our mission: participate within mainstream commercial markets, derive meaningful benefits as a symbol of empowerment and transformation. We currently support 85 vulnerable households and 204 orphans/children with daily food and services.

14 hectares Leruleng (owned PTO)
4 hectares Madiga (leased)

Our Agricultural Production

Mixed farming: crops, broilers, layers & organic practices

Crop Production

Maize (150,000 roundup ready), cabbage, onions, carrots, beetroot, tomatoes, spinach & potatoes. Using natural herbs for pest control and chicken dropping for organic fertilizer.

Dryland + irrigation planned

Poultry (Broilers & Layers)

2000 broilers + 3000 point-of-lay hens. Egg production: ~1200 eggs/day (approx. 20 dozen daily). Hatchery unit, battery cages, deep litter system, solar backup.

Organic & Sustainable

Recycling poultry waste as fertilizer, moisture-conserving mulch, natural herbs for insect control, and plans for greenhouse with 10,000 seedling capacity.

Beyond Farming: Uplifting Sebayeng

Our cooperative directly supports the Tiangmaatla Multipurpose Centre's drop-in services for orphans and vulnerable children affected by HIV/AIDS and poverty.

  • Daily meals for 204 vulnerable children
  • Skills training in poultry & crop farming
  • Door-to-door poverty alleviation campaigns
  • Local market supply: hawkers, spaza shops, schools
“We identified poverty levels stressing Sebayeng — farming is the solution.” – Mrs. Matsi M.M, Director
